I'm Doris Odei, a development professional working at the intersection of data and policy. Growing up in a peri-urban community in Ghana, I saw firsthand how policy gets distorted when the right data is never collected, and when the data we do have never informs decisions, an experience that has driven my work ever since. With an interdisciplinary background spanning economics, sociology, population studies, and survey & data science, I help programs sharpen decision-making by combining instrument design, causal inference, statistical modeling, and expert knowledge. Over five years across West Africa and the United States, I've connected rigorous data collection to real-world impact, helping programs measure what matters and act on what they find. I hold an MSc in Survey & Data Science from the University of Maryland, College Park, and an MA in Population Studies from the University of Ghana. I'm particularly interested in how survey methodology and data quality shape the evidence behind social programs, and how getting that right leads to more equitable, impactful decisions.
