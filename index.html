I am a development professional with an interdisciplinary background in economics, sociology, population studies, and survey & data science. Growing up in a peri-urban community in Ghana, I experienced how neglected data shapes and distorts policy, and that experience drives my work.

Over five years across West Africa and the US, I have worked at the intersection of rigorous data collection and real-world impact, helping programs measure what matters and act on what they find. I am particularly interested in how survey methodology and data quality shape the evidence behind social programs, and how getting that right leads to more equitable decisions.
